Why these numbers
Pinterest posts compound. Every pin is a permanent search result pointing to your Etsy listing. High frequency here is always worth it — much of it can be repurposed from Instagram graphics.
Instagram 3–4x builds consistent presence without demanding daily creation. Carousels and static graphics from a single batch session can cover a full week.
Threads is low-lift — it's thinking out loud. A single observation or behind-the-brand thought takes minutes. This is your consistency anchor when life gets busy.
Pillar distribution (per week)
The Origin Story — 3–4 posts (your conversion engine, always on)
Winging It to Winning It — 3–4 posts (your growth engine, prioritise reach platforms)
Behind the Brand — 2–3 posts (mostly Threads, minimal production lift)
Glow Up Proof — 1–2 posts (grows naturally as the brand gets traction)
Batch Workflow
One session, one week of content
The goal is a single focused session that fills Metricool for the week. Follow this order — each step feeds the next.
The sequence
1
Pick your pillar focus for the week
Choose 1–2 pillars to lead with. Rotate weekly so no pillar goes stale.
2
Write the Threads posts first (10–15 min)
These are your raw thoughts. They'll become captions and graphic copy downstream.
3
Expand 2–3 Threads posts into Instagram captions
Add context, a hook, and a CTA. The thinking is already done — you're just dressing it.
4
Build 2–3 Instagram graphics or carousels
Pull the strongest line from each caption as the visual headline. Canva templates speed this up.
5
Repurpose Instagram graphics as Pinterest pins
Add a keyword-rich pin title and description. Link directly to your Etsy listing. One graphic → 2–3 pins with different descriptions.
6
Schedule everything in Metricool
Threads: spread across the week. Instagram: 3–4 slots. Pinterest: daily or every other day. Done.
Time estimate
If it feels like play: 2–3 hours covers the full week across all three platforms
If it feels like work: split into two 45-minute sessions — write on day one, design and schedule on day two
